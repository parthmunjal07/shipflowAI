# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: [Current Date]**

This Privacy Policy explains how ShipFlow AI ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you visit our website or use our application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
When you register for ShipFlow AI, we may collect personal information such as:
- Name
- Email address
- Profile picture (via OAuth providers like GitHub or Google)

### Technical & Usage Data
We automatically collect certain information when you visit, use, or navigate the application. This information does not reveal your specific identity but may include device and usage information, such as your IP address, browser and device characteristics, operating system, referring URLs, and information about how and when you use our services.

### Integration Data
If you connect ShipFlow AI to third-party services (such as GitHub):
- We receive a unique installation ID to interact with the third-party API on your behalf.
- We **do not** permanently store your source code. Code diffs are processed ephemerally in memory to provide AI code reviews.
- We store metadata related to your issues, pull requests, and code snippet comments specifically generated by our platform.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect or receive to:
- **Provide and maintain our services:** Including user authentication, tenant isolation, and billing.
- **Improve our platform:** Understanding usage patterns to enhance user experience.
- **Communicate with you:** Sending administrative information, security alerts, and support responses.
- **Process payments:** Facilitating subscription management via third-party processors (e.g., Razorpay, Stripe).

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We only share information with third parties in the following situations:
- **Service Providers:** We may share data with vendors who perform services for us (e.g., AI models like OpenAI/Mistral, payment processors, hosting providers). *Note: We enforce strict agreements prohibiting AI vendors from using your data to train their models.*
- **Business Transfers:** In connection with a merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition.
- **Legal Obligations:** If required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

## 4. Data Security & Retention

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process.
- All data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
- We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy.
- Upon account deletion, all relational data is permanently cascaded and deleted from active databases, and purged from backups within 30 days.

## 5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:
- The right to access, update, or delete your information.
- The right to restrict or object to our processing of your information.
- The right to data portability.

To exercise these rights, please contact our support team.
